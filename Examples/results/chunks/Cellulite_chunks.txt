The Endocarditis Team The importance of an Endocarditis Team in the diagnosis, management, and clinical outcomes of patients with IE has been demonstrated in several observational studies.Establishing multidisciplinary endocarditis teams according to the European Society of Cardiology (ESC) and the American College of Cardiology/American Heart Association Guidelineshas resulted in earlier and more accurate diagnosis of the primary disease and its complications,uniform antibiotic treatment,and optimized timing for surgical intervention.A variety of scenarios of patients presenting with IE justifies a multidisciplinary approach. Furthermore, the clinical presentation may vary significantly depending on the characteristics of the host and virulence of the microorganism. Accordingly, the concept of the Endocarditis Team needs to embrace a multidisciplinary approach that must adapt according to the patient’s clinical needs and the local epidemiology to ensure prompt diagnosis and treatment. The members of the Endocarditis Team should include the specialists with direct involvement in the diagnostic and therapeutic processes (Table 7), and may vary depending on the type of centre.
