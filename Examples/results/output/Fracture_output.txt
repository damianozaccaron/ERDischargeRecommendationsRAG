SECTION 1 — DISCHARGE RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Take paracetamol 1 g orally as needed for pain.
2. Keep the plaster cast on your left ankle clean, dry and intact, and do not remove it yourself.
3. Put weight on your left leg as much as you can tolerate.
4. A prescription is issued for orthopaedic evaluation for follow-up of the left lateral malleolus fracture and management of the plaster cast.
5. Go to the nearest emergency department immediately if you experience swelling of the whole leg or calf, pain along the leg, sudden shortness of breath or chest pain, numbness, tingling, coldness or bluish colour of the toes, or pain not controlled by the prescribed therapy.
6. You are referred back to your general practitioner for any further diagnostic or therapeutic needs and ongoing care.

SECTION 2 — CLINICAL REFERENCES (for the reviewing physician)
1 - (NonComplexFractures, p.6-6) (Clinical note) - Oral paracetamol is recommended for mild fracture pain, and 1 g orally was started in the emergency department.
2 - (Clinical note) - A plaster cast was applied by the orthopaedic team and must stay intact until orthopaedic review.
3 - (NonComplexFractures, p.8-9) - Immediate unrestricted weight-bearing as tolerated is advised in the non-surgical management of unimalleolar ankle fractures.
4 - (NonComplexFractures, p.8-9) (Clinical note) - Orthopaedic follow-up is advised for unimalleolar ankle fractures, and the orthopaedic team took over care with a 30-day prognosis.
5 - (SevereLimbTraumaAcuteCare, p.10-10) (VenousThrombosis, p.9-9) - Ankle fracture with cast immobilisation carries a moderate VTE risk, and leg swelling and tenderness along the deep veins are signs of DVT.

2. Mantenga l'apparecchio gessato della caviglia sinistra pulito, asciutto e integro, e non lo rimuova da sola.

4. Si rilascia impegnativa per visita ortopedica per il follow-up della frattura del malleolo laterale sinistro e la gestione dell'apparecchio gessato.
5. Si rechi immediatamente al pronto soccorso più vicino in caso di gonfiore di tutta la gamba o del polpaccio, dolore lungo la gamba, improvvisa mancanza di respiro o dolore toracico, intorpidimento, formicolio, freddezza o colorito bluastro delle dita del piede, oppure dolore non controllato dalla terapia prescritta.
6. Si rimanda al medico di medicina generale per ogni ulteriore necessità diagnostica o terapeutica e per il proseguimento delle cure.
