WOMAN, 27 YEARS OLD
Medical history:
- Pathological proximate: The patient presented following a single-joint injury to the left ankle. She has not taken any pain relief. She denies having sustained a head injury.
- Remote pathology: Non-smoker. No known medical conditions. Denies any drug allergies. Categorically denies being pregnant.

Objective examination:
The patient is alert, oriented and cooperative. She arrives in a wheelchair; unable to walk. Pale, non-tender swelling of the lateral malleolus of the left ankle. The metatarsals and the remaining bony structures of the left leg are non-tender. No current vascular or neurological deficits.

Clinical diary:
- 1 g of paracetamol administered orally
- The patient is accompanied to the radiology department for an X-ray of the left tibiotarsal joint.
- Radiological images are reviewed: compound spiral fracture of the left lateral malleolus.
- Orthopaedic management: a plaster cast is applied.

Diagnosis: fracture of the left peroneal malleolus
Prognosis: 30 days, barring complications
