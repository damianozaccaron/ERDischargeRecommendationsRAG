Male, 42 years old
Medical history:
- Pathological proximate: The patient presents with retrosternal chest pain in the supine position present for about 3 days, not exacerbated by breathing or movement. He complains of dry cough for several weeks. Denies fever or dyspnoea.
- Remote pathology: No known cardiovascular events nor family history. Not affected by diabetes. Normal weight. Former smoker of about half a pack per day, STOP ten years ago. Previous helicobacter pilori infection, eradicated about 5 years ago. Otherwise no known diseases. Denies drug allergies.

Objective examination: Vesicular breath sounds present in all lung fields, no added sounds. Heart sounds normal-rate, rhythmic and with no murmurs. Soft abdomen, not painful on superficial or deep palpation. CVA Tenderness and Blumberg negative. Cystic points non-tender. No dependent oedema or signs of venous thrombosis.

Clinical diary:
- Venous access obtained and performance of troponin plus biochemistry, complete blood count and pro-BNP. ECG performed: sinus rhythm, no signs of ST segment depression/elevation.
- Arterial blood gas performed: physiological exchanges
- The patient is sent to radiology for performance of chest X-ray in 2 projections.
- The tests are reviewed: troponin <14 therefore negative. No inflammatory markers. Chest X-ray negative
for acute findings.
- 40mg of pantoprazole in 100cc saline solution is administered.
