FEMALE, 23 YEARS OLD
Medical history:

Blumberg’s sign negative. CVA Tenderness negative. No dependent oedema in the lower limbs. Blood pressure 112/70 mmHg. Heart Rate 94 bpm. SpO₂ 99% on oxygen. Body Temperature 36.6°C.

Clinical diary:

Right adnexa: a round para-ovarian mass of approximately 2 cm, with heterogeneous content, suspected to be an ectopic gestational sac. A modest amount of free fluid in the Douglas pouch. Left adnexa normal.

Diagnosis: suspected ectopic pregnancy
