# ER Discharge Recommendations

Generates guideline-grounded discharge recommendations for Italian Emergency Room patients. A curated corpus of official European clinical guidelines is the sole source of clinical knowledge; the LLM organises and expresses it, it does not supply it.

## Language

### Inputs and outputs

**Clinical Note**:
The Italian ER document a physician writes during a visit: medical history, objective examination, clinical diary, diagnosis, prognosis. The system's only patient-side input.
_Avoid_: letter, discharge document, patient data

**Guideline**:
One official European clinical guideline, as published by a medical society. The unit of the corpus.
_Avoid_: document, source, PDF

**Recommendation**:
One actionable instruction addressed to the patient or their GP, generated by the system and reviewed by a physician before use.

### Corpus units

**Chunk**:
A stored passage of a Guideline, carrying its source Guideline and page range. The unit of retrieval.
_Avoid_: passage, segment, block

**Prose Chunk**:
A Chunk built from consecutive running-text sentences of a Guideline.

**Sentence Row**:
A Chunk built from one row of a Sentence Table: its Table Description, the row's sentence, and the row's short cells in brackets (class, level). Lives in the same index as Prose Chunks.

**Sentence Table**:
A Guideline table whose rows are sentences with a few short cells beside them, typically a recommendation table. Its rows become Sentence Rows; the table itself is not stored.

**Grid Table**:
A Guideline table whose cells are values under column headers: doses, criteria, scores. Stored whole in the Table Store as markdown, or as parts split where its Group changes when too long. Follows the table across page breaks; a row is never cut. Classified by cell shape, never by wording.

**Group**:
The section line a Grid Table places above a run of rows ("Beta-blockers"), carried by every row beneath it. Rendered as the first column.

**Table Surrogate**:
The short text standing in for a Grid Table or one of its rows during retrieval. The table's (Table Description, headers, Groups, key cells) is embedded for the broad query; each row's (Table Description, Group, header: cell pairs) is what the cross-encoder scores. The markdown is what the LLM reads.

**Table Store**:
The Grid Tables with their surrogates and token sets, beside the Chunk index. Searched in a second hop, after the Excerpts are known and only for a home discharge, by the medications in the Clinical Note (weighed more) and in the Excerpts. A table scores its best row and takes one slot with its best part.

**Evidence Table**:
A Guideline table listing trial-level evidence behind a recommendation, or any listing of studies (first author, study type, population). Carries no guidance for the patient; not ingested, and its text is kept out of the prose too.

**Rejected Table**:
A region the table finder mistook for a table: a single row or column (a box), a header or leading cell of paragraph length, or mostly running text. Handed back to the prose pipeline rather than dropped; so is a reference list laid out like a table, whose references the prose pipeline removes while the running text beside them stays. A region laid out like a table that holds no guidance (a table of contents, an abbreviation list) is not a Rejected Table: it is dropped with its text.

**Table Description**:
The caption or title stating what a table is about, e.g. "Drugs for rate control in AF". Belongs to the table, not to the surrounding prose, wherever it is set: above, below or beside the table, or as its first row. Found by its label ("Table 3", "Box 1", "Recommendation Table 19"), never by publisher. Shown to the LLM as written; a cleaned form (no trailing parenthetical, no reference markers) feeds the Table Surrogate.
_Avoid_: caption (as a general term; captions of figures are discarded, not described)

**Excerpt**:
A Chunk once selected by retrieval and presented to the LLM with a citation identifier [E#]. The same content as a Chunk, in its cited role.

**Cited Table**:
A Grid Table once selected by the Table Store hop and presented to the LLM with a citation identifier [T#]. Dosing may be copied from it, never computed.
